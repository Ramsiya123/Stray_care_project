About

The Stray Reporting and Adopting System is a system for reporting aggressive, injured, tired stray and wild animals and abandoned pets in our surroundings to concerned authorities. The system has a provision for kind hearts to adopt them. The system is a prototype that is assumed to be working by an Animal Welfare Organization.

The system is a good solution for the current scenario of repeated dog attacks, when we see malnutritioned domestic animals like cows on roadsides which are abandoned by their owners, injured animals suffering after an accident.and when the wild animals coming to villages near forests. We are somewhat helpless in many of those situations. Our heart tells us to do something but we have nothing to do.

At least we have that caring mind for the surrounding beings around us. On the other hand we have a lot of Organizations around us who spend wealth and health for these animals. Our initiative is to connect these assets of our society, the hearts that care for the suffering animals and an organization which is ready to adopt and foster them. The adopted animals can later be transferred to caring hands.

This is a system which helps the civilians to be safer from violent animals as it provides a reporting system which will connect to the nearby government office. It is a mercy for animals as it provides a provision to report wounded animals to nearby government veterinary hospitals and they are later fostered by the organization. Villagers can instantly report wild animals to forest officials with pictures. We can also report abuses towards animals with evidence to the police.
